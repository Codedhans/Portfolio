Meazura

**Meazura**is a professional digital management tool for tailors and designers, developed by **CodedHans**. It replaces paper-based records with a secure, mobile-first platform for tracking client body measurements.
The Problem:Traditional paper notebooks are disorganized, insecure, and hard to search.
The Solution: A centralized digital vault that prioritizes data privacy, entry speed, and professional organization.
Key Features:

  • Dynamic Profiles:
    • Create and edit detailed client measurement records.
  • Local-First Privacy:
    • Data is stored securely in the browser, keeping sensitive info private.
  • CSV Backup:
    • One-click export allows users to download their entire database for spreadsheet use.

Professional UI:Responsive dark/light mode interface designed for shop-floor utility.
Tech Stack: HTML5, CSS3 (Custom Variables), and Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+)
